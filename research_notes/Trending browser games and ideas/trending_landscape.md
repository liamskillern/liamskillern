## 1. Which daily puzzle games are hot now (NYT, LinkedIn, indie "dle" games)?

### Takeaway
The daily-puzzle format is still the dominant browser-casual format, but the energy in late 2026 is with independent daily games, led by Krillion (a rare-answer trivia game), not with new Wordle clones. NYT's 2026 addition (Crossplay) is a multiplayer app, and LinkedIn added two games in 2026 (Patches in March, Wend in June). On an independent weekly chart of daily web games, Wordle (#22) and Connections (#26) now sit well below indie titles.

## 2. What is "Krillion"?

### Takeaway
Krillion is real: it's the breakout browser game of late 2026. It's a free daily trivia game where you type open-ended answers to seven prompts and rarer valid answers score more, framed as diving to the ocean floor ("One in a Krillion" is the top rarity tier). A pseudonymous solo dev ("kp") made it. It went viral through schools and social media, holds #1 on the Playlin 40, and has spawned many clones, answer sites, and copycats (a Minecraft version, Ireland's Boreen).

### Cited Findings
- How it plays: each day has seven prompts shared by all players, with about 25 seconds per prompt (one site says 20s, another says a 3-second preview plus 25s). You type answers yourself (no multiple choice), and many answers are valid. "Common answers float, rare ones sink you to the trench floor" — [TechWiser answers page](https://techwiser.com/krillion-answers-today/); [Game Rant, dive #131](https://gamerant.com/krillion-answers-today-november-23-dive-131/); [playlin.io game page](https://playlin.io/game/krillion/) (every point = 10 meters deeper); [krillion.org](https://www.krillion.org/) (says 20s).
- Scoring: points rise with rarity, but answers must be accepted, so obscure invalid answers score 0. Rarity tiers include "One in a Krillion" (100 pts) and "Deep Cut" (85 pts). One site cites 700 max daily points — [dlegames.org](https://dlegames.org/game/krillion); [Parodybox](https://www.parodybox.net/krillionio); tier data from answer sites ([puzzit](https://puzzit.com/answers/krillion), [wordanswer.today](https://wordanswer.today/krillion-answers)), which conflict with each other.
- Daily numbering: dive #131 fell on Nov 23, 2026 — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/krillion-answers-today-november-23-dive-131/). One answer site reported 10,618 accepted answers across 6 tiers for that dive (unverified, other sites conflict) — search summary of answer sites.
- Monetization (per a guide site): the daily dive is free, and endless dives plus the archive are a one-time $5 unlock. Another site calls it free with no ads — [dailydles.com](https://dailydles.com/games/krillion) (snippet; fetch blocked); [krillion.to](https://krillion.to/en).
- Creator: a single person going by "kp," pseudonymous. The official FAQ reportedly added a section because others were claiming to be the creator and an AI search result named a clone's maker as the creator — [krilliondaily.com "Who Made Krillion?"](https://krilliondaily.com/blog/who-made-krillion) (a fan/SEO site summarizing the FAQ).
- Original vs. clones: the original appears to be krillion.io. krillion.org says it is "independently operated and not affiliated with Krillion.io." A fan Minecraft edition references "the daily rare-answer game at krillion.io." Many mirrors exist (krillion.to, krillion.cc, krilliondaily.org, gplus.im, topgames.gg) — [krillion.org](https://www.krillion.org/); [endgametools Minecraft Krillion](https://endgametools.com/en/minecraft/news/minecraft-krillion); [krillion.cc](https://krillion.cc/).
- Popularity: a student newspaper reports it "absorbing and gaining thousands of new players daily over the past few months," estimating "hundreds of thousands of players daily, if not millions," big in schools and bigger on social media — [IBW Headlight "Krillion: Is this the new big game?"](https://ibwheadlight.com/7600/artsentertainment/krillion-is-this-the-new-big-game/); [Scot Scoop "Krillion takes a dive into classroom games"](https://scotscoop.com/krillion-takes-a-dive-into-classroom-games/). The Irish Times reportedly puts it at "hundreds of thousands" daily — [Irish Times](https://irishtimes.com/life-style/people/2026/11/22/whats-the-story-with-boreen-the-daily-irish-trivia-game) (search snippet). All are estimates, with no developer figure.
- Chart: #1 on the Playlin 40 for the week of Nov 16, 2026, 15 weeks on the chart — [Playlin 40](https://playlin.io/charts/).
- Mainstream answer-guide coverage (Game Rant, TechWiser) now publishes daily Krillion answers, the same treatment Wordle/Connections get — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/krillion-answers-today-november-23-dive-131/); [TechWiser](https://techwiser.com/krillion-answers-today/).
- A Wikipedia ".io games" snippet says Krillion "has spurred a popularity surge in daily .io games since early 2026" — [Wikipedia: .io games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.io_games) (search summary only; couldn't verify wording).

### Inferences
- Counting back from dive #131 on Nov 23, 2026, daily dive #1 was around mid-July 2026 (if numbering is one per day with no gaps). That fits "15 weeks on chart" (since early August). This is inferred, not stated by a source.
- Why it spread: (1) the rare-answer twist rewards knowledge breadth rather than one correct word, which makes the results interesting to compare and share. (2) It takes about 3 minutes. (3) Classroom play drove it among students. (4) The tier names ("One in a Krillion") are easy to brag about. These points come from the cited descriptions; the causal ranking is my inference.
- The large number of clone and SEO domains around the name is itself a marker of strong search demand.

### Gaps
- No official launch date, developer interview, or verified DAU/MAU.
- Couldn't confirm krillion.io as the canonical URL from a first-party page, since fetches were blocked. It is inferred from krillion.org's disclaimer and the Minecraft fan page.
- No mainstream tech-press (Verge/Polygon/Kotaku/NYT) coverage of Krillion surfaced in search.

## 3. Other viral browser games of 2025–2026 (outside daily puzzles)

### Takeaway
The viral non-daily hits of 2026 are mostly novelty or social-commentary web toys spread on social media and HN: Your AI Slop Bores Me (humans role-playing as AI, March 2026), Destroy Any Website (Sept 2026), and Messenger (a cozy multiplayer WebGL walking sim, 2025, re-viral June 2026). There are also regional satire hits (Fightkaro, India, Nov 2026). No strong 2026 evidence surfaced for a new Infinite Craft-scale AI game or a breakout .io or idle title.

### Cited Findings
- **Your AI Slop Bores Me**: launched around March 7, 2026 at youraislopbores.me. Players LARP as an AI answering other users' prompts, earn tokens, and spend them to submit prompts to the "AI" (other humans). The name riffs on the "Your Politics Bore Me" meme. It was built by Mihir Maroju per Know Your Meme, Kotaku and Tom's Guide (another site's attribution conflicts) and went viral after an HN launch — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/your-ai-slop-bores-me); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/ai-llm-slop-new-free-game-2000677087); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-tried-the-viral-website-where-humans-pretend-to-be-chatgpt-and-its-surprisingly-fun); [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/haha-now-im-the-ai/); [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/your-ai-slop-bores-me-game). A "50M views in one week" claim appears only on a promotional clone page and is unverified. Numerous pages.dev clones exist.
- **Destroy Any Website**: a free browser game by Hugo Duprez/Sprite Fusion that turns any URL into a destructible 2D stickman level, released September 2026 — [aiidelist explainer](https://aiidelist.com/blog/destroy-any-website) (secondary source).
- **Messenger** (Abeto; devs Vicente Lucendo and Michael Sungaila): a free WebGL game, released in 2025, where you deliver mail on a tiny planet and other live players are visible (emoji-wave interactions). Abeto calls it "one of our most viral projects, with millions of impressions." It went viral again around a June 15, 2026 tweet, with notice from Yoko Taro and Ikumi Nakamura — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/messenger-browser-game-abeto); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/deliver-mail-on-tiny-colorful-planet-in-this-relaxing-web-game); [Gigazine (Sept 2025)](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250929-messenger-browser-game); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/this-viral-web-browser-game-is-so-good-players-cant-believe-its-free-3376111/).
- **Fightkaro / "Sansad Showdown"**: a fan-made browser political-satire fighting game with fighters resembling Indian politicians, trending on social media in early November 2026 — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/literally-fight-as-indian-politicians-fightkaro-a-new-political-satire-game-gaining-traction-on-social-media-4205714).
- **Astrocade "Speed Per Step"**: a 3D obstacle course where you speed up with each step, named in a 2026 viral listicle. Astrocade is an AI game-creation platform. Low-quality source — [primepulselogic listicle](https://primepulselogic.com/browser-games-2026-viral-trend/).
- **Portal evergreen titles** named in 2026 listicles (SEO-grade, unranked): Monkey Mart, Crazy Motorcycle, Cowpocalypse, Dude Simulator, Poppy Playtime-style horror, Pumpkin Panic, Red Face — [financialcontent/businesnewswire (Oct 2026)](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/businesnewswire-2026-10-1-the-most-viral-games-everyone-is-playing-online-this-year); [ottergames TikTok list](https://ottergames.org/blog/most-viral-browser-games-on-tiktok/) (snippet only).
- **AI-coded indie games**: HN's "Ask HN: Show your AI coded games [June 2026]" thread shows a wave of hobbyist browser games built with LLM coding tools (e.g., a naval strategy game built with Claude Opus, and a sphere chess variant built for about $100 of prompting). There was also a Show HN "Lumberjack" automation game satirizing AI layoffs — [HN Ask thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48509243); [Show HN: Lumberjack](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50535647).
- **Background**: One Million Checkboxes (2024) remains the reference point for "massively shared web toy" virality — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Million_Checkboxes).

### Inferences
- The viral 2026 pattern is "a one-line hook you can explain in a tweet" plus social or commentary value: anti-AI sentiment (Your AI Slop Bores Me), destroying the web (Destroy Any Website), local politics (Fightkaro), or cozy, beautiful presence with strangers (Messenger).
- AI shows up more as a production tool (cheap AI-coded games, Astrocade) and as a theme (AI fatigue) than as an LLM-in-the-loop mechanic. Krillion's creator faced questions about AI use in answer lists, which suggests players are sensitive to it.

### Gaps
- No 2026 data found on Infinite Craft or successor AI-crafting games, new .io hits, idle/incremental breakouts, merge or physics viral games (Suika-likes), or GeoGuessr's own numbers.
- No verified traffic numbers for any of the viral titles above.

## 4. Which platforms and portals matter now, with stats?

### Takeaway
Poki and CrazyGames remain the big web portals (company claims: about 100M and 60M+ monthly users respectively; third-party estimates are much lower). YouTube Playables is the fastest-growing new distribution channel (1,669 games, 50+ markets). LinkedIn and NYT anchor "habit" daily puzzles. Telegram mini-app gaming has faded since its 2024 bubble. Discord Activities and Reddit Devvit are active but lack public numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **Poki**: reported 625M players in 2025 and claims about 100M monthly active players (announced March 2026, without outside funding) — [tech-insider comparison](https://tech-insider.org/ie/free-games-online-poki-vs-crazygames-vs-itch-io-2026/) (secondary).
- **CrazyGames**: claims 60M+ monthly users and 3B gameplays in 2026 (developer docs previously said 50M+). A Semrush-based estimate gives about 35M MAU, about 300M monthly gameplays, and a Similarweb global rank near #473. Some independent analyses put both Poki and CrazyGames nearer 30M MAU each — [tech-insider: CrazyGames vs Poki vs Coolmath](https://tech-insider.org/crazygames-vs-poki-vs-coolmath-games-2026/); [Cinevva publishing guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-web-game). Company vs. third-party numbers conflict, and definitions differ (uniques vs. sessions).
- An indie dev case study describes earning about $15,000 from one Three.js game on CrazyGames — [froessell substack](https://froessell.substack.com/p/15000-from-one-browser-game-the-math).
- **YouTube Playables**: 1,669 live games on Sept 23, 2026, with a median of about 290K lifetime plays per game. Brain & Puzzle has the largest share of plays (23.6%) and games (37.3%). Simulation and Racing are overrepresented relative to game count — [Dubit tracker](https://dubit.io/library/how-many-plays-does-a-youtube-playables-game-get). It launched in 2024 in 7 markets and was in 50+ countries by Sept 2026, with no IAP — [Shattered.io](https://shattered.io/youtube-playables-50-markets-zero-iap-2026/). Monetization is planned to expand beyond the US in summer 2026 — [Mediacube](https://mediacube.io/en-US/blog/youtube-playables-updates). There's no official MAU.
- **Discord Activities**: most Activities are now free for all users, with no Nitro needed (third-party guide) — [peakbot guide](https://peakbot.pro/blog/discord-activities-apps-guide-2026). No official usage stats were found.
- **Reddit Devvit**: Reddit ran a "Game of the Day 2026" hackathon on Devvit Web, producing many daily games — [GitHub examples](https://github.com/GaiaSG94/gameday-rebus). No player stats were found.
- **Telegram mini-apps** (fading): Hamster Kombat lost about 259M users (an 86% drop to 41M MAU) by Nov 2024 as PAWS drew 20.5M users in 8 days. Notcoin and Catizen also fell sharply. Q4 2024 saw broad revenue and retention trouble — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/hamster-kombat-decline-telegram-game-competition-rise-of-paws); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/web3/2025-02-07-telegram-games-had-trouble-earning-revenue-retaining-users-in-q4-report-339563). For 2026, one promotional TON blog says Catizen had about 13M MAU in April 2026 and that "the hype peak is long over" (unverified) — [dev.to TON adoption](https://dev.to/ton-adoption/hamster-kombat-what-remains-after-the-hype-in-2026-4c1n).
- **Daily-game aggregators** have emerged as their own discovery layer: Playlin ("Where Daily Games Live," a weekly chart), dlegames.org, dailyplay.org, and dailydles — [Playlin](https://playlin.io/); [dailyplay.org](https://dailyplay.org/); [dlegames.org](https://dlegames.org/game/krillion).
- **Distribution channels** for 2026 daily games: WhatsApp groups (Boreen), classrooms and student social media (Krillion), Bluesky (Playlin's account), HN (Your AI Slop Bores Me), X/Twitter (Messenger's re-virality) — sources as cited in sections 1–3.
- An industry map of the web-games sector exists (Playgama 2026) — [Playgama market map](https://wiki.playgama.com/playgama/articles/introducing-the-web-games-industry-market-map-2026) (not read in full).

### Inferences
- For a new daily game, discovery now runs through word of mouth (share grids, group chats, classrooms) plus aggregator charts and answer-guide SEO, not portals. Portals (Poki/CrazyGames) matter for arcade, .io, and session-based games. YouTube Playables suits puzzle/brain games.

### Gaps
- No 2026 itch.io browser-game stats, no Discord Activities MAU, no Devvit player figures, no NYT/LinkedIn engagement numbers.
- Poki/CrazyGames figures are self-reported, and no independent audit exists.

## 5. Which genres and formats are rising and which are fading?

### Takeaway
Rising: open-answer and rarity-scored daily trivia (the Krillion wave), logic and spatial daily puzzles, geography dailies (MapTap), short one-hook social web toys, puzzle games on YouTube Playables, and AI-assisted indie development. Fading: Telegram tap-to-earn games, plain Wordle clones (still huge in absolute terms but slipping on indie charts), and crypto-gated web games.

### Cited Findings
- Rising: rare-answer daily trivia. Krillion is #1 on Playlin for 15 weeks, and imitators include Boreen (Ireland) and the Minecraft Krillion fan edition. "Games like Krillion" guides exist — [Playlin 40](https://playlin.io/charts/); [Irish Times](https://irishtimes.com/life-style/people/2026/11/22/whats-the-story-with-boreen-the-daily-irish-trivia-game); [endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/minecraft/news/minecraft-krillion); [krillionguide similar games](https://krillionguide.com/en/similar-games).
- Rising: logic puzzles over word games at LinkedIn (Patches) — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=319165). Clues by Sam reached 50K+ DAU by early 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam).
- Rising: geography dailies (MapTap praised as stickier than Worldle/Globle) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/maptap-a-daily-geography-game-is-my-new-wordle).
- Rising: multiplayer and social features in daily-puzzle brands (NYT Crossplay with chat and matchmaking) — [WTOP/AP](https://wtop.com/consumer-news/2026/01/this-just-in-from-the-new-york-times-its-first-2-player-game-and-an-evolving-business-model/).
- Rising: brain and puzzle games on YouTube Playables (23.6% of plays) — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/library/how-many-plays-does-a-youtube-playables-game-get).
- Relative slide: Wordle at #22 and Connections at #26 on Playlin's indie-skewed chart — [Playlin 40](https://playlin.io/charts/). This is relative to indie games on one aggregator, not an absolute decline.
- Fading: Telegram tap-to-earn (see section 4) — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/hamster-kombat-decline-telegram-game-competition-rise-of-paws).
- Indie devs changing the Wordle formula in 2026 (e.g., Gridling, WordTwiq, MultaVerba) — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/liza_18827c15bdd387c4cd9c/building-a-game-like-wordle-in-2026-five-ways-indie-developers-changed-the-formula-3412); [ravenware: new word games 2026](https://ravenware.dev/blog/new-word-games-2026).

### Inferences
- The winning daily format in 2026 is one shared daily run, about 3 minutes, scored on a continuum (rarity, distance, depth) rather than pass/fail, with a brag-worthy share result. Krillion, MapTap, and Size It Up / Extraplot by name all fit this. Scored-on-a-continuum is my inference from the descriptions.
- There's a counter-AI cultural current (Your AI Slop Bores Me, players asking whether Krillion uses AI), so "human-made" may be a selling point.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on idle/incremental, .io, or merge/physics genre trends in 2026. Wikipedia's .io page reportedly notes a daily .io surge since early 2026, but that is unverified.
- No Google Trends data was retrieved.
